# TikTok rules that decide whether this stream gets reach, and how we comply

Last checked: 26 October 2026. Sources: TikTok Community Guidelines (effective 24 Sep 2026), TikTok Creator Academy originality policy, TikTok LIVE guidelines, TikTok Shop LIVE quality rules (May 2026), and several 2026 creator-tooling write-ups of the enforcement rules. Where TikTok does not publish a number, it says so below.

## 1. Unoriginal / reproduced content (the restriction you already hit)

**What TikTok says.** Content "copied completely from others", "largely repurposed from another source without adding any creative edits", "combined from multiple sources with little or no additional information and/or content value", or carrying "someone else's visible watermark or superimposed logo" is unoriginal. Such content "is ineligible for the For You feed". For LIVE specifically: LIVE sessions that "stream unoriginal content without new or creative edits" are not eligible for the For You feed, and unoriginal content is named first among the reasons for losing Creator Rewards eligibility ("copied from others or has minimal original input or edits").

**What moderation actually flags on LIVE (observed enforcement, not published thresholds):** playing pre-recorded video "even your own", screen recordings, "playing content from another device", slideshows, looping footage, black/blank/blurred screens, long absences from camera, on-screen text or QR codes from other platforms, "repeated or prolonged actions that lack clear objectives or direct interaction with viewers", and no-face streams treated as "low interaction".

**How we comply (built in):**
- Every pixel is generated by us: our own charts (TradingView's open-source renderer with our data), our own room scene, our own captions. No clips, no screen recordings of other apps, no watermarks.
- Nothing loops. Lessons rotate on a 72-hour cooldown, phrase banks never repeat back to back, the market tour uses live data, the clock is real.
- Real-time interaction: viewer questions answered by name within the lesson, welcomes, follow and gift thanks, chat prompts, hooks for newcomers.
- A permanent on-screen disclosure that the host is AI, and the host says so when asked. TikTok's 2026 AI rules require labelling realistic AI content; unlabelled realistic AI content is reported to lose a large share of reach within 48 hours.

**Residual risk you must accept:** an unattended stream with no human face can still be judged "low interaction" by a reviewer. The two strongest mitigations are (a) you appearing on the stream for part of the day, and (b) chat volume: the more real questions the host answers, the safer the stream.

## 2. Music on LIVE (your "viral songs" plan)

**What TikTok says.** "You may not post, upload, stream, or share content that infringes upon another party's intellectual property rights, including copyrighted music, without proper authorization." Neither the in-app Sound Library nor the Commercial Music Library is cleared for LIVE broadcasts. Copyrighted background music is the number one cause of muted LIVEs and copyright strikes, and a remix, speed-up or cover of a known song is still that song's composition: it is matched and treated the same, and deliberately altering audio to evade matching is "circumvention", which carries a heavier penalty than the original violation.

**What happens if we do it anyway:** audio muted mid-stream, the LIVE ended, a copyright strike on the account, and repeated strikes end LIVE access. That is the opposite of traction.

**How streamers play popular songs legally:** TikTok LIVE Studio has its own Music tab (the note icon): a library TikTok has cleared for live broadcasting, mixed in by TikTok with a volume slider. That is the only way to play well-known music, Arabic or otherwise, without the fingerprint muting it. It is separate from our audio, so pick a track there, set its volume low under the voice, and leave our music bed off (`MUSIC_LEVEL=0`) when you use it. Anything played from outside (files, Spotify, YouTube, speed-ups, pitch shifts) is matched within 30 to 60 seconds.

**What we do instead (built in):**
- A music bed that plays only files in the `music/` folder, at a low level, ducked under the voice.
- `scripts/make_music.py` generates original beats locally (`--style arabic` for oud, darbuka, khaleeji, shaabi and dabke flavoured instrumentals) in the styles that travel on TikTok (afrobeats, amapiano, reggaeton, melodic trap, phonk, dance pop). They are ours, nothing to match.
- If you want recognisable human-made tracks, use a library whose license explicitly names TikTok LIVE streaming (Epidemic Sound and similar sell that), and keep the license file.

## 2b. The community group (Telegram)

The Chart Room has a free Telegram group for discussion, follow-up questions and chart screenshots. Haddan mentions it
on a schedule (`app/brain/community.py`: at most once between lessons every ~30 minutes and once inside a newcomer hook
every ~50 minutes, a hundred different wordings, none repeated within a day). Every mention says what the group is not:
no signals, no calls, no paid anything, nobody tells anyone what to trade. That promise is what keeps the mention inside
TikTok's financial rules; the moment the group shares trade calls, the mentions become the thing that gets the LIVE
ended and the account struck, and the compliance filter in `app/brain/filters.py` is written to block any sentence that
pairs a group with signals. `COMMUNITY_MENTIONS=0` in `.env` turns the mentions off.

## 3. Financial content

TikTok prohibits promoting financial scams, "get-rich-quick" schemes, guaranteed returns, and treats crypto and forex promotion as restricted. Educational content is the permitted lane. The host never gives signals, targets, predictions or income claims (system prompt plus a regex filter), never links out, and says "not financial advice" on screen at all times.

## 4. Engagement manipulation

"Content that tries to manipulate people into giving gifts or inflating likes or follows isn't eligible for the For You feed." The host thanks gifts and follows, and may ask for likes once in a while in a normal creator way, but never conditions content on gifts, never runs "gift to unlock" mechanics, and never begs.

## 5. Age, eligibility, LIVE access

LIVE requires 18+ and, in most regions, 1,000 followers. LIVE monetization requires following the LIVE Monetization Guidelines; unoriginal content is an explicit disqualifier.

## 6. Daily checklist before pressing Go LIVE

1. `.env` has your handle, so chat is read and answered.
2. `music/` contains only owned or LIVE-licensed files.
3. The stage window shows moving charts, the clock, captions and the disclosure banner.
4. The AI-content disclosure is enabled in LIVE Studio where offered.
5. You plan to drop in on camera at least once during the day if you can.
